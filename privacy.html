3. In short

Our apps have no user accounts and we run no servers of our own. Your progress (high score, best streak, in-game currency, daily streak, number of rounds played and settings such as sound on/off) is stored in the app's local storage on your device only. 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The apps are funded by ads served through Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

Google processes your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, and your interactions with ads. This happens for ad delivery, frequency capping, performance measurement, fraud prevention and, only with your consent, personalised advertising.

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, collected on first launch through Google's UMP consent form. Without consent you are shown non-personalised ads. You can change or withdraw consent at any time by tapping WERBUNG at the bottom of the app's main menu.

Google also processes data in the United States, based on the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision.
