2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to every mobile app published under the developer name JL Studios, on Google Play and on any other store we publish to. It is deliberately written for all of them at once rather than per app, so there is one current version and nothing can drift out of date.

One policy works for all of them because they are all built the same way:

  • no user account, no login, no profile
  • no servers of our own, so nothing is sent to us
  • everything the app remembers stays in storage on your device
  • funded by advertising through Google AdMob

If an app ever does more than this, that app will say so in its own store listing, and a section will be added here before it ships. Until such a section exists, exactly what follows applies.

3. The short version

We do not collect personal data. We have no servers to collect it with and no accounts to attach it to. Your progress stays on your phone.

Our apps do show ads, and to show them Google AdMob processes data about your device. That is the only data collection involved, it is done by Google rather than by us, and section 5 describes it.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our apps are free and are funded by ads served through Google AdMob.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

Data processed - by Google, not by us: your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), IP address, device type, operating system version, language setting, approximate location derived from the IP address, and your interactions with ads such as impressions, clicks and completed videos.

Purposes: delivering ads, limiting how often you see the same one, measuring ad performance, detecting fraud and invalid traffic, and - only if you consent - personalising ads.

Legal basis: your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, collected on first launch through Google's UMP consent form. If you do not consent, you are shown non-personalised ads and the app stays fully playable.

Changing or withdrawing consent: open the app's main menu and tap the advertising link in the footer, usually labelled ADS. Google's consent form opens again and your earlier choice can be changed at any time.

Transfers outside the EU: Google also processes data in the United States, on the basis of the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision.

More from Google:

  • Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Ad providers that may serve ads: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

Resetting or deleting your advertising ID: Android Settings, then Privacy, then Ads. The exact path depends on the manufacturer. Deleting the ID stops it being used to personalise ads.

Our apps contain no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no attribution SDKs and no social media plugins. We do not build profiles and we do not track you across apps or websites. From Google we receive only aggregated, non-personal reporting about ad revenue.

9. Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the EU or the EEA you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Consent you have given can be withdrawn at any time, with effect for the future.

Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data have to go to Google, who is the controller for it. For anything else, contact us using the details in section 1.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. The one responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Germany.

10. Your rights in the United States

If you live in California or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law, the following applies.

We do not sell your personal information for money and never have. Sharing your advertising ID with Google for the purpose of personalised advertising may, however, count as selling or sharing under the CCPA/CPRA and comparable laws.

You can opt out in either of two ways, and neither requires contacting us: decline personalised advertising in the consent form described in section 5, or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Opting out does not reduce what the app can do; you simply see non-personalised ads.

You also have the right to know what is collected, to request deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. As we hold no personal information ourselves, requests about advertising data should go to Google.

11. How long data is kept

We keep nothing, so there is nothing on our side to delete. On-device data lives until you uninstall the app or clear its data. How long Google keeps advertising data is set out in Google's own privacy policy, linked in section 5.
